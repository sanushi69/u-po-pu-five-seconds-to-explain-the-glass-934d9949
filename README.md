Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Five Seconds to Explain the Glass"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-five-seconds-to-explain-the-glass-934d9949).

## The entry

`main.py` writes one sentence — the sentence a house cat would say in the
five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Under the Household Treaty, anything on the table's edge is mine to inspect, and that glass failed its audit.

109 characters, one sentence, in the cat's own voice. No apology, no
confession — just the most convincing case the cat can make for itself.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
